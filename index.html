how it works

01

Copy the post link

Open the post on X, tap the share icon, and choose Copy link. Or just copy it from the address bar.

02

Paste it above

Drop the link into the field and hit Pull video. Pullwire reads the post's public data — no login required.

03

Choose a rendition

Posts often carry several encodes. Pick the resolution you want and save it straight to your device.

good to know

Where does the file come from?

Every download is fetched directly from X's own video CDN (video.twimg.com). Pullwire doesn't store, host, or re-encode any video — it only locates the link that's already public on the post.

Does this work on private accounts?

No. Pullwire can only read what's publicly viewable without logging in. Protected accounts and posts removed by the author won't resolve.

Can I download GIFs too?

Yes. What X displays as a "GIF" is technically a looping MP4 — Pullwire pulls it the same way as a regular video.

Is this free?

Yes, no account or watermark. Running the lookup and CDN bandwidth does cost money, so if it's useful to you, sharing it helps keep it up.
